GapDetail

Archived owners 23 owners are archived = true in owners.csv but are active users in HubSpot. Kept active by decision.

MQL goal progress The 3 goals are loaded, but HubSpot calculates progress from its own MQL entry dates, which show the load date. Progress in HubSpot won't match the data; use rl_date_became_mql in the BI. Goals are assigned to Zachary Wood, the Marketing owner who created them in the source.
HubSpot deal lifecycle sync HubSpot automatically moves companies and contacts linked to a Closed Won deal to customer.
