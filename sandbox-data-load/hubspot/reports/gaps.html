3. Differences from the source

GapDetail
Create date on contacts and companies HubSpot treats it as read-only, so the native Create date shows 2026-10-18. The original date is in rl_source_createdate. Deals keep their original create date.
Archived owners 23 owners are archived = true in owners.csv but are active users in HubSpot. Kept active by decision.
Duplicate emails 4 contacts are loaded without an email, because another contact already has it. The email is kept in rl_duplicate_email.
MQL goal progress The 3 goals are loaded, but HubSpot calculates progress from its own MQL entry dates, which show the load date. Progress in HubSpot won't match the data; use rl_date_became_mql in the BI. Goals are assigned to Zachary Wood, the Marketing owner who created them in the source.
HubSpot deal lifecycle sync HubSpot automatically moves companies and contacts linked to a Closed Won deal to customer. It changed 3 companies the source keeps at opportunity; they were set back, and the loader restores lifecycle stages after every load. Records may change again if deals are edited by hand.

2. Differences from the source

GapDetail
Record owners The trial has 5 user licences for 45 source owners, so every record is owned by the loading user, as the guide says. The source owner ID is in Ridgeline_Owner_Id__c; the 77 source users are in Ridgeline_User__c.
Created date on campaigns and products Salesforce doesn't allow setting it on these objects. The original is in Ridgeline_Created_Date__c. Accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities and campaign members keep their original created date.
Lead conversion date Converted leads show the load date as the conversion date. The original is in Ridgeline_Converted_Date__c.
Last modified date Shows the load date on the 2,278 converted accounts and 2,098 converted opportunities, because the conversion writes to them. 29 accounts have a last modified date earlier than their created date in the file, which Salesforce rejects; they use the created date.
Campaign member first responded date Set by Salesforce from the member status. The original is in Ridgeline_First_Responded_Date__c.
Trial org expiry The org expires on 2026-11-20. Reload into a new trial before then; the loader does the whole setup.
