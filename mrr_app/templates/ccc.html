How the cash conversion cycle is calculated (spec CB-10)

  • DSO = AR(e) ÷ Revenue_q × D · DIO = Inventory(e) ÷ COGS_q × D · DPO = AP(e) ÷ COGS_q × D · CCC = DSO + DIO − DPO
  • Balances at the quarter's last month e ({{ 'by account' if info.mode == 'accounts' else 'by Balance Sheet section until the resync' }}): AR = {{ info.members.ar|join(', ') or '-' }}; inventory = {{ info.members.inventory|join(', ') or 'none' }}; AP = {{ info.members.ap|join(', ') or '-' }}.
  • Revenue and COGS for the quarter from the EBITDA classification; COGS {{ 'includes' if info.include_da else 'excludes' }} D&A in cost of sales ({{ info.cogs_da_names|join(', ') or 'none' }}). D = calendar days in the quarter (92 for Q3).
  • Quarterly by definition. "Each month" uses the 3 months ending that month and that month-end's balances. $ view: working capital = AR + inventory − AP.
