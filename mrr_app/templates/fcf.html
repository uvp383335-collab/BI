How FCF conversion is calculated (spec CB-07)

  • OCF = Net income + D&A − Δ working capital. Net income and D&A ({{ info.da_names|join(', ') }}) use the EBITDA classification. ΔWC = [({{ info.wc_assets }}) − ({{ info.wc_liabs }})] at e minus the same at e − 12{% if info.wc_mode == 'accounts' %}; AR = {{ info.ar_names|join(', ') or 'no account' }}{% else %} (by Balance Sheet section until the resync){% endif %} - set in Settings.
  • Capex = {% if info.mode == 'accounts' %}increase in the month-end balances of {{ info.capex_names|join(', ') }}{% if info.accum_names %}; accumulated D&A not counted: {{ info.accum_names|join(', ') }}{% endif %}{% if info.excluded_names %}; excluded: {{ info.excluded_names|join(', ') }}{% endif %}{% else %}change in the {{ info.sections|join(' + ') }} total + D&A charged on those assets ({{ info.fa_da_names|join(', ') }}){% endif %} - spec: 1510 + 1520, the QuickBooks "Investing activities" lines.
  • FCF conversion % = (OCF − capex) ÷ EBITDA, each summed over the trailing 12 months. "Single month" shows the same for one month - noisy because of A/R timing.
  • Months after the last month with P&L revenue ({{ info.last_closed or '-' }}) and Balance Sheet months identical to the month before (not synced yet) are left out.
