How recurring revenue % is calculated (spec VC-12)

  • Recurring %(W) = 100 × Σmonths Σcustomers MRR (USD at each month's rate) ÷ GL revenue(W) for a month, a quarter, or the trailing 12 months.
  • MRR = subscription + retainer MRR per customer, exactly as on the MRR page (product mapping, annual / quarterly invoices spread, CAD converted at each month's rate).
  • GL revenue = the P&L income accounts (QuickBooks demo accounts excluded).
  • Account-tagged % = revenue on {{ info.tagged|join(' + ') or 'the tagged accounts' }} ÷ GL revenue - a data-quality contrast: it misses retainers booked to 4120 Professional Services and includes implementation bundled into subscription lines.

Higher is better. Compared with the Oryon report, the MRR basis runs 0.5–2 points lower mainly because QuickBooks has no service credits or write-offs, so GL revenue is higher; the account-tagged basis matches. The report's region filter (GL Location) isn't available in the monthly P&L snapshot.
