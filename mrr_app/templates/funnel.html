How funnel conversion is calculated (spec CM-06)

  • MQL → SQL (headline) = contacts whose "{{ info.mql_stage }}" date is in the cohort and that have a "{{ info.sql_stage }}" date ÷ those contacts.
  • Lead → MQL = marketing-sourced{{ ', non-duplicate (one per email)' if info.dedupe }} contacts created in the cohort that have an MQL date ÷ those contacts.
  • SQL cohort = contacts with an SQL date in the cohort {{ 'that also have an MQL date (came through the funnel - the Oryon report\'s rule)' if info.sql_via_mql else 'from the marketing sources' }}. SQL → opportunity = of those, contacts with an "{{ info.opp_stage }}" date; opportunity → won = of those, on a won deal; SQL → won = SQL cohort on a won deal ({{ info.won_contacts }} contacts on won deals).
  • Marketing-sourced = original source in {{ info.marketing|join(', ') or 'nothing selected' }}. Stage dates from the lifecycle-stage history, never the current stage.
  • TTM pools 12 cohort months (sum converted ÷ sum cohort). Immature: cohort ended fewer days before {{ info.cutoff }} than the step's maturity period. Region / segment filters aren't available - contacts have no region or account link.
