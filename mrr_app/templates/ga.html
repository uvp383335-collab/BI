How G&A % is calculated (spec VC-09)

  • G&A %(W) = 100 × Σ G&A ÷ Revenue(W) for a month, a quarter, or the trailing 12 months (TTM = sum of 12 months' G&A ÷ sum of 12 months' revenue).
  • G&A = the ticked accounts: {{ selected|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') }}.
  • Excluded: one-time transaction & deal fees (6490) and D&A (6510, 6520).
  • Revenue = the income accounts{% if excluded_rev %}, without the excluded QuickBooks demo accounts ({{ excluded_rev|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') }}){% endif %} - the same revenue as the COGS % page.

Lower is better. Compared with the Oryon report, quarters run 0.1–0.5 points lower mainly because QuickBooks has no service credits or write-offs, so revenue is a little higher; single months can differ by a few points because some G&A costs land in a different month, which evens out over a quarter.
