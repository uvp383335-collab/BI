How revenue growth is calculated (spec VC-13)

  • Growth(W) = 100 × (Revenue(W) ÷ Revenue(W − 1 year) − 1) - W is a month, a quarter, or the trailing 12 months; W − 1 year is the same months a year earlier.
  • Revenue added = Revenue(W) − Revenue(W − 1 year).
  • Recurring YoY = the same on summed customer MRR (as on the MRR page); Non-recurring YoY = the same on revenue − MRR.
  • Vs plan = 100 × (Revenue(W) ÷ plan total revenue(W) − 1), plan from Settings.
  • vs last year's growth = this growth − the growth of the same window a year earlier (in points).

Higher is better. Growth needs a year of history, so it starts 12 months after the first P&L month ({{ info.first or '-' }}). Region and product filters aren't available - the monthly P&L snapshot has no Location or Class.
