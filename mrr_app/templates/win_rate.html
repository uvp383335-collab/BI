How win rate is calculated (spec CM-03)

  • Deals: {{ info.provider }} deals of type {{ (info.nb_types or [])|join(' / ') or '-' }} that are closed (won or lost{{ ' - from the pipeline stage definitions' if info.stage_defs else ' - stage Closed Won / Closed Lost' }}) with a close date in the trailing {{ window }} months ending at the month.
  • Competitor: {{ info.std.get(info.comp_field, 'custom field "' ~ info.comp_field ~ '"') }}. Losses with loss reason {{ info.exclude_reasons|join(', ') }} ({{ 'field "' ~ info.loss_field ~ '"' if info.loss_field else 'no loss-reason field set' }}) are not decided.
  • Win rate vs c = 100 × won ÷ decided, skipped when nothing was decided. Logged = deals with any competitor ÷ all closed New Business deals in the window.
  • Quarterly shows the quarter-end months only (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec), each with its trailing {{ window }}-month window - the Oryon report's view. Monthly rolls the same window every month.
  • Segment filter: the Salesforce account's segment. Region isn't synced, so there's no region filter.
