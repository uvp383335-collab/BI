How LTV / CAC is calculated (spec VC-07 and VC-14)

  • Monthly churn c = 1 − (GRRTTM ÷ 100)1/12 - company TTM GRR ({{ 'subscription only' if info.sub_only else 'all recurring revenue' }}), same as the Retention page.
  • Expected lifetime = min(1 ÷ c, {{ info.cap|int }}) months; "cap" marks windows where the cap binds (ltv_capped).
  • LTV = new-logo ARR ÷ (new logos × 12) × GMTTM × lifetime - new logos, new-logo ARR and GM as on the CAC page.
  • LTV / CAC = LTV ÷ CAC, CAC = TTM S&M ÷ TTM new logos. Higher is better; below {{ info.threshold }}x is a warning.
  • Whole base (VC-14): LTVbase = ARR(e) ÷ (active customers(e) × 12) × GMTTM × lifetime - the same lifetime and CAC, with the average MRR of all customers with MRR > 0 at e (ARR and customers as on the MRR page) in place of new-logo MRR.

Equivalent: LTV / CAC = lifetime ÷ CAC payback. The latest quarter is "incomplete" while deals closed in it haven't billed yet - CAC is too high until the next invoice month arrives. The Oryon report's values between quarter ends don't follow this formula, so compare on quarter ends.
