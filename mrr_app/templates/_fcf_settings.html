CB-07FCF conversion (capex accounts)

FCF conversion = (operating cash flow − capex) ÷ EBITDA, trailing 12 months (spec CB-07). Net income, D&A and EBITDA come from the EBITDA account classification; capex and working capital come from the Balance Sheet snapshots in {{ bs_collection }}. Spec capex = 1510 Computer Equipment + 1520 Capitalized Software Development - the "Investing activities" lines on the QuickBooks Statement of Cash Flows.
