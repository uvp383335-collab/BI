How lead volume vs plan is calculated (spec CM-08)

  • MQLs vs plan = 100 × (MQLs ÷ plan − 1); the count view is MQLs − plan.
  • MQLs = {{ info.provider }} contacts whose first "{{ info.mql_stage }}" date (lifecycle-stage history) falls in the period - a contact recycled back to MQL counts once.
  • Plan = the Budget sheet's MQL target per quarter (Settings); a month carries a third of its quarter. TTM sums 12 months of MQLs and plan.
  • Region / segment filters aren't available - contacts have no region or account link.
