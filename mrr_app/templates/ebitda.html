How EBITDA margin is calculated (spec VC-10)

  • EBITDA(W) = Revenue − COGS − Opex + D&A; EBITDA margin = 100 × EBITDA ÷ Revenue - for a month, a quarter, or the trailing 12 months.
  • COGS includes capitalized-software amortization; Opex = sales, marketing, customer success, R&D, G&A (including one-time deal fees) and D&A.
  • D&A added back: {{ da_names|join(', ') or 'none selected' }}. Deal fees are not added back.
  • Below EBITDA (not counted): {{ below|join(', ') or 'none' }}.
  • Vs plan = margin − plan margin, plan margin = Σ plan EBITDA ÷ Σ plan revenue over the period's months (a month uses that month's plan).
