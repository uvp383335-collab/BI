How customer concentration is calculated (spec CM-02)

  • Groups: {% if info.group_parent %}Salesforce account (QBO_Customer_Id__c link, else name match) walked up ParentId to the top; else the QuickBooks parent customer; else the customer itself{% else %}every QuickBooks customer on its own (parent grouping off){% endif %}. Now: {% for k, v in info.hows.items() %}{{ v }} {{ k }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}.
  • Recognized revenue per group over the 12 months ending at e: annual subscription items ({{ info.annual_items|length }} items mapped "Subscription - annual") spread 1/12 per month from the service start - the invoice month, or the next month when dated on or after the early-invoice day; every other line in its invoice month. Canadian customers converted at the monthly CAD→USD rate.
  • Top-{{ info.top_n }} share = 100 × Σ the {{ info.top_n }} largest groups ÷ Σ all groups; largest = the top group's share. Invoiced basis: invoice totals by invoice date over the same 12 months.
  • Product lines = the QuickBooks class on each invoice line: {{ info.classes|join(', ') }}. Lines without a class are in "No class"; every line counts in "All classes".
