How COGS % is calculated (spec VC-04)

For each window W (a month, a quarter, or the trailing 12 months):

  • COGS % = 100 × COGS(W) ÷ Revenue(W). COGS = every COGS line including capitalized-software amortization ({{ da_names|join(', ') or 'none selected' }}); revenue = every income line. Accounts set to "Exclude" in Settings are left out.
  • Excl. capitalized software = 100 × (COGS − amortization) ÷ Revenue.
  • Platform / Services / Hardware COGS % = 100 × that product line's COGS ÷ that product line's revenue.
  • Platform share of revenue = 100 × Platform revenue ÷ total revenue.

Product lines come from the account mapping in Settings, because the monthly P&L snapshot has no QuickBooks Class. The Oryon report splits by Class and leaves COGS posted without a class "unallocated" (about $8k a month for Ridgeline); here those costs land on the product line of their account, so Services and Platform COGS % run slightly higher than the report. Lower is better.
