How CAC and payback are calculated (spec VC-06)

For every month e, over the 12 months ending at e:

  • S&M = sum of the ticked expense accounts in plmonthlysnapshots (expenses[].amounts.Total).
  • New logos and new-logo ARR = customers whose MRR goes above $0 for the first time, by close month (first billing month − {{ info.lag }}), ARR = first-month MRR × 12 - the same list as the New logos page.
  • Gross margin = (income − COGS) ÷ income, summing the income[] and cogs[] lines.
  • CAC = S&M ÷ new logos.
  • CAC payback (months) = CAC ÷ ((new-logo ARR ÷ new logos ÷ 12) × gross margin). Lower is better; above {{ info.threshold }} months is a warning.
  • S&M per $1 of new ARR = S&M ÷ new-logo ARR.

A month is "incomplete" when its window has deals that close after {{ info.complete_until }} (they start billing after the last invoice month, {{ info.last_invoice }}), starts before {{ info.first_valid_close }} (customers already paying when the data begins would look new), or is missing P&L months.
