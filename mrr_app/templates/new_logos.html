How new-logo revenue is calculated

New-logo ARR(period) = Σ over new customers whose close month is in the period of (first-month MRR × 12)  ·  Growth = (this period − same period last year) ÷ same period last year × 100
  1. Customer MRR for every month, exactly as on the MRR page (monthly lines, annual ÷ 12, quarterly ÷ 3, retainers; CAD at the month's rate).
  2. New logo = a QuickBooks customer whose MRR is above $0 for the first time. Customers who had MRR before, dropped to $0 and came back are win-backs, not new logos.
  3. Date = the Salesforce close month = first billing month − {{ info.lag }} (Settings → New-logo billing lag). Deals closed in the last {{ info.lag }} month{{ 's' if info.lag != 1 }} before {{ info.last_invoice }} haven't billed yet, so those periods are marked incomplete.
  4. New-logo ARR = the first month's MRR × 12. For an annual deal that is the first-year contract value (annual line ÷ 12 × 12).
  5. Salesforce account: joined on qboCustomerId (QBO_Customer_Id__c) when it's set, otherwise QuickBooks displayName = Account name after normalizing (case, punctuation, Inc/LLC/Corp/Co, and the parent part of "Parent:Child"). The account gives the segment; unmatched customers are "Unlinked".
  6. Subsidiaries: {{ 'a new customer whose Salesforce parent group already had MRR is expansion and is left out.' if info.group_parent else 'a new customer under an existing Salesforce parent still counts as a new logo (same as the Oryon report, VC-03). Change this in Settings.' }}
  7. Data start: customers already paying when the invoice data begins only appear at their first renewal, up to 12 months later, so new logos are counted from {{ info.first_valid_close }}.
