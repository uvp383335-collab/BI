How cash and runway are calculated (spec CB-05)

  • Cash(e) = unrestricted cash at the end of month e = {% if info.mode == 'accounts' %}the month-end balances of {{ info.chosen|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') }}{% if info.restricted_accts %}; restricted, not counted: {{ info.restricted_accts|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') }}{% endif %}{% if info.excluded_bank %}; excluded: {{ info.excluded_bank|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') }}{% endif %}{% else %}the {{ info.sections|join(' + ') }} section total{% if info.adjust %} minus {{ info.adjust|usd }} restricted cash{% endif %}{% endif %} - spec: 1010 + 1015 + 1020, restricted cash 1030 excluded.
  • Runway = Cash(e) ÷ ((Cash(e − {{ info.lookback }}) − Cash(e)) ÷ {{ info.lookback }}) - cash divided by the average monthly decline over the last {{ info.lookback }} months.
  • When cash did not fall over those months, runway is null and shows Self-funding.

Point in time: "At month end" or "At quarter end". "Not closed" = after the last month with P&L revenue ({{ info.last_closed or '-' }}).
